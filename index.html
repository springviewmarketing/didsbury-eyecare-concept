Eye care

Clinical care for every age.

An eye examination does far more than check your prescription. It can pick up early signs of glaucoma, retinal detachment and cataracts, and even general health problems such as diabetes and raised blood pressure.

Eye examinations

A full optometric service with fully qualified optometrists. Everyone over 40 should have regular checks, especially if a relative has glaucoma.

Children’s eye tests

Designed to be fun and relaxed, with paediatric optometry from two years old. We advise a first eye examination at around three, and NHS eye tests are free for under-16s.

Medical eye care

Cataract assessment and direct referral, glaucoma screening, ocular hypertension referral refinement and diabetic screening. OCT imaging and interpretation at Didsbury.

About us

Looking good, feeling happy, seeing clearly.

It started with Didsbury Eyecare on Wilmslow Road. Owner and optometrist Gary Lesser has since opened Stalybridge Eyecare on Melbourne Street and Alderley Eyecare in the heart of Alderley village.

Across all three, our fully qualified optometrists and experienced dispensing team share one aim: that you leave looking good, feeling happy and with perfect vision. Every member of staff is trained in dispensing, so you’ll get help finding a frame that suits your face, your lifestyle and your budget.

Long-standing relationships with the best optical manufacturers give us access to most of the world’s finest frames, while our specialist glazing laboratory stocks an extensive range of lenses, including ultra-thin options for strong prescriptions.
